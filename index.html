South Ruchi - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

South Ruchi ("the game") is a restaurant simulation game published by FinCloud Developers. This policy explains what information the game uses.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email address or phone number. Your game progress (levels, cash, hotel name and settings) is saved only on your device.

Advertising

The game shows ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob and its partners may collect and use device identifiers such as the advertising ID, IP address and general usage data to show and measure ads and prevent fraud. Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK) the game asks for your consent first. You can reset or turn off personalised ads in your device settings. See Google's policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Data deletion

Because progress is stored on your device, you can delete it by clearing the game's data or uninstalling it.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
